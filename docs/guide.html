STEP 1

Logging In

  1. Open your browser and go to the Invoice Hub login page.
  2. Select your name from the dropdown list.
  3. Enter your 4-digit PIN.
  4. Click Sign in.
Figure 1: Login screen — select your name and enter PIN
Tip If you don't see your name, contact your administrator to set up your account.
STEP 2

Dashboard

After signing in, you'll see your dashboard with summary statistics and a list of your previous invoices.

Figure 2: Dashboard — your invoice overview
STEP 3

Open My Profile

In the left sidebar, click My Profile. This page shows your personal and payment details.

Figure 3: My Profile — manage your details
STEP 4

Update Personal Details

On the My Profile page, you can update your:

  • Full name
  • Email
  • Phone
  • ABN

Edit any field directly, then click Save Profile.

Tip Your name and ABN appear on invoices as the contractor identity.
STEP 5

Update Payment Details

Scroll down to the Payment details section and fill in:

  • Bank name
  • BSB
  • Account number

Click Save Profile.

Figure 4: Payment details section — fill in your bank information
Warning Payment details appear on invoices under PAYMENT DETAILS. Make sure they're complete before sending.
STEP 6

Change Your PIN

  1. Scroll down to the Security section.
  2. Enter your Current PIN.
  3. Enter your New PIN (exactly 4 digits).
  4. Confirm your new PIN.
  5. Click Save Profile.
Figure 5: Security section — change your PIN
Tip Leave the PIN fields blank if you don't want to change your PIN.
Warning Your current PIN is required to change your PIN. Never share your PIN.
STEP 7

Create a New Invoice

In the sidebar, click New invoice. The invoice form opens with:

  • Invoice details (number, email, company name, address)
  • Weekly wage calculator
  • Dates & Notes
Figure 6: New invoice form
STEP 8

Select Work Days (Weekdays)

The wage calculator shows Monday–Friday. For each day, choose:

OptionAmount
Off$0
Half$90
Full$180
Figure 7: Wage selector showing Monday Full, Tuesday Half, Wednesday Off, Thursday-Friday Full, Saturday Half
Tip The selector clearly shows your choice. No more guessing!
STEP 9

Select Saturday (Optional)

Saturday has two options:

OptionAmount
Off$0
Half$100
Figure 8: Saturday selector — Off or Half ($100)
Note Saturday is always a fixed $100 half-day. There is no full-day option.
STEP 10

Use Quick Actions

Three shortcuts to quickly set common patterns:

ButtonWhat It Does
Select Mon–FriMon–Fri = Full ($180 each), Sat = Off
Select Mon–SatMon–Fri = Full ($180 each), Sat = Half ($100)
Clear WeekAll days = Off
Figure 9: Quick action buttons
STEP 11

Check the Weekly Total

The Week Total displays at the bottom of the calculator. It updates automatically as you select or deselect days.

Figure 10: Week total showing $820.00 (Mon-Thu full, Sat half)
STEP 12

Add Dates & Notes

  1. Select the Week starting date.
  2. Dates & Notes shows the dates for each day.
  3. You can adjust individual dates if needed.
  4. Add any Notes that should appear on the invoice.
Tip Dates automatically populate based on your selected week. Click a day tile to cycle through states.
STEP 13

Save Draft

  1. Click Add to invoice to add the wage line item.
  2. Verify the line item appears (description: "Wages/Retainer — Week of...").
  3. Click Save draft to save without sending.
Figure 11: Save your invoice as a draft
Tip Saving as a draft lets you come back and edit later.
STEP 14

View Your Invoices

Click Dashboard in the sidebar to see all your invoices. Click any invoice to view it.

Figure 12: Invoice history on dashboard
STEP 15

Edit a Draft Invoice

From the dashboard, click the invoice you want to edit. On the invoice view page you can continue editing, download PDF, or send & email.

Note Only drafts can be edited. Sent invoices are locked.
STEP 16

Download the PDF

Open the invoice (draft or sent) and click Download PDF. The PDF downloads to your computer.

Figure 14: Download the invoice PDF
STEP 17

Save & Email

Complete your invoice with wage details, then click Save & email. The system:

  1. Saves the invoice
  2. Downloads the PDF
  3. Opens an email with the invoice details pre-filled
Figure 14: Save & email your invoice
Warning Make sure your bank details (Payment Details) are complete before sending. Invoices without payment details may delay processing.
