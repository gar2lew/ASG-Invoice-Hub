STEP 1

Logging In

  1. Open your browser and go to the Invoice Hub login page.
  2. Select your name from the dropdown list.
  3. Enter your 4-digit PIN.
  4. Click Sign in.
Desktop login screen
Mobile login screen
Tip If you don't see your name, contact your administrator to set up your account.
STEP 2

Dashboard

After signing in, you'll see your dashboard with summary statistics and a list of your previous invoices.

Desktop dashboard
Mobile dashboard with bottom navigation
Tip On mobile, use the bottom navigation bar (Dashboard, New Invoice, My Profile) to move between pages.
STEP 3

Open My Profile

Click My Profile in the navigation (left sidebar on desktop, bottom nav on mobile).

My Profile page
STEP 4

Update Personal Details

On the My Profile page, you can update your:

  • Full name
  • Email
  • Phone
  • ABN

Edit any field directly, then click Save Profile.

Tip Your name and ABN appear on invoices as the contractor identity.
STEP 5

Update Payment Details

On the My Profile page, fill in:

  • Bank name
  • BSB
  • Account number

Click Save Profile.

Payment details section
Warning Payment details appear on invoices under PAYMENT DETAILS. Make sure they're complete before sending.
STEP 6

Change Your PIN

  1. Scroll to the Security section on My Profile.
  2. Enter your Current PIN.
  3. Enter your New PIN (exactly 4 digits).
  4. Confirm your new PIN.
  5. Click Save Profile.
Security section - change your PIN
Tip Leave the PIN fields blank if you don't want to change your PIN.
Warning Your current PIN is required to change your PIN. Never share your PIN.
STEP 7

Create a New Invoice

Click New invoice in the navigation. The invoice form opens with invoice details, weekly wage calculator, and Dates & Notes.

New invoice form (desktop)
New invoice form (mobile)
STEP 8

Select Work Days (Weekdays)

The wage calculator shows Monday-Friday. For each day, choose:

OptionAmount
Off$0
Half$90
Full$180
Wage selector showing Monday Full, Tuesday Half, Wednesday Off
Tip The selector clearly shows your choice with distinct colors for each state.
STEP 9

Select Saturday (Optional)

Saturday has two options:

OptionAmount
Off$0
Half$100
Saturday selector - Off or Half ($100)
Note Saturday is always a fixed $100 half-day. There is no full-day option.
STEP 10

Use Quick Actions

Three shortcuts to quickly set common patterns:

ButtonWhat It Does
Select Mon-FriMon-Fri = Full, Sat = Off
Select Mon-SatMon-Fri = Full, Sat = Half
Clear WeekAll days = Off
Quick action buttons
STEP 11

Check the Weekly Total

The Week Total displays at the bottom of the calculator. It updates automatically as you select or deselect days.

Week total showing calculated amount
STEP 12

Add Dates & Notes

  1. Select the Week starting date.
  2. Dates & Notes shows the dates for each day.
  3. You can adjust individual dates if needed.
  4. Add any Notes that should appear on the invoice.
Tip Dates automatically populate based on your selected week. Tap/click a day tile to cycle through states.
STEP 13

Save Draft

  1. Click/tap Add to invoice to add the wage line item.
  2. Verify the line item appears (description: "Wages/Retainer - Week of...").
  3. Click/tap Save draft to save without sending.
Save your invoice as a draft
Tip Saving as a draft lets you come back and edit later.
STEP 14

View Your Invoices

Go to Dashboard (left sidebar on desktop, bottom nav on mobile) to see all your invoices. Tap/click any invoice to view it.

Invoice history on dashboard
STEP 15

Edit a Draft Invoice

From the dashboard, tap/click the invoice you want to edit. On the invoice view page you can continue editing, download PDF, or send & email.

Note Only drafts can be edited. Sent invoices are locked.
STEP 16

Download the PDF

Open the invoice (draft or sent) and click/tap Download PDF. The PDF downloads to your computer or device.

Download the invoice PDF
STEP 17

Save & Email

Complete your invoice with wage details, then click/tap Save & email. The system saves the invoice, downloads the PDF, and opens an email with the invoice details pre-filled.

Save & email your invoice
Warning Make sure your bank details (Payment Details) are complete before sending.

TIP

Switching Between Light and Dark Theme

You can switch the appearance of the Invoice Hub:

  • Desktop: Click the theme button in the bottom-left corner
  • Mobile: Tap the theme icon in the top-right corner
Tip Your theme preference is saved automatically and will persist when you return.
TIP

Mobile Navigation

On phones and tablets:

  • Top header shows "Invoice Hub" branding and the theme toggle
  • Bottom navigation provides quick access to Dashboard, New Invoice, and My Profile

The app adapts automatically to your screen size.
