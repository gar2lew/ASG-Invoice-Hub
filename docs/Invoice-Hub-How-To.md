# How to Use the ASG Invoice Hub

A simple step-by-step guide for sales representatives.

---

## Step 1: Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to the Invoice Hub login page.
2. Select your name from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN.
4. Click **Sign in**.

> **Tip:** If you don't see your name, contact your administrator to set up your account.

![Login Screen](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/01-login.png)

---

## Step 2: Dashboard

After signing in, you'll see your dashboard with:
- Summary statistics (week-to-date, all-time, drafts, sent invoices)
- A list of your previous invoices

![Dashboard](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/02-dashboard.png)

---

## Step 3: Open My Profile

1. In the left sidebar, click **My Profile**.
2. This page shows your personal and payment details.

![My Profile](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/03-my-profile.png)

---

## Step 4: Update Personal Details

On the **My Profile** page, you can update:

- **Full name**
- **Email**
- **Phone**
- **ABN**

1. Edit any field directly.
2. Click **Save Profile** at the bottom.

> **Tip:** Your name and ABN appear on invoices as the contractor identity.

---

## Step 5: Update Payment Details

Scroll down to the **Payment details** section:

- **Bank name**
- **BSB**
- **Account number**

1. Fill in or update your bank details.
2. Click **Save Profile**.

> **Warning:** Payment details appear on invoices under PAYMENT DETAILS. Make sure they're complete before sending.

![Payment Details](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/04-payment-details.png)

**Payment status indicator:**
- **Complete** — all bank fields filled
- **Missing information** — some fields empty

---

## Step 6: Change Your PIN

Scroll down to the **Security** section:

1. Enter your **Current PIN**.
2. Enter your **New PIN** (exactly 4 digits).
3. **Confirm** your new PIN.
4. Click **Save Profile**.

> **Tip:** Leave the PIN fields blank if you don't want to change your PIN.

> **Warning:** Your current PIN is required to change your PIN. Never share your PIN.

![Change PIN](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/05-change-pin.png)

---

## Step 7: Create a New Invoice

1. In the sidebar, click **New invoice**.
2. The invoice form opens with:
   - Invoice details (number, email, company name, address)
   - Weekly wage calculator
   - Dates & Notes

![New Invoice](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/06-new-invoice.png)

---

## Step 8: Select Work Days (Weekdays)

The wage calculator shows Monday–Friday. For each day, choose:

| Option | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| **Off** | $0 |
| **Half** | $90 |
| **Full** | $180 |

1. Click the day name (Mon, Tue, etc.) to reveal the options.
2. Select **Off**, **Half**, or **Full**.

> **Tip:** The selector clearly shows your choice. No more guessing!

![Wage Selector](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/07-wage-selector.png)

**Example:** Monday Full + Tuesday Half + Wednesday Off
- Monday: $180
- Tuesday: $90
- Wednesday: $0
- **Running total: $270**

---

## Step 9: Select Saturday (Optional)

Saturday has two options:

| Option | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| **Off** | $0 |
| **Half** | $100 |

1. Click the **Sat** day.
2. Select **Off** or **Half**.

> **Note:** Saturday is always a fixed $100 half-day. There is no full-day option.

![Saturday Options](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/08-half-day-example.png)

---

## Step 10: Use Quick Actions

Three shortcuts to quickly set common patterns:

| Button | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Select Mon–Fri** | Monday–Friday = **Full** ($180 each), Saturday = **Off** |
| **Select Mon–Sat** | Monday–Friday = **Full** ($180 each), Saturday = **Half** ($100) |
| **Clear Week** | All days = **Off** |

1. Click the button.
2. All days update automatically.

![Quick Actions](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/09-quick-actions.png)

---

## Step 11: Check the Weekly Total

The **Week Total** displays at the bottom of the calculator.

- Updates automatically as you select/deselect days
- Shows the breakdown (e.g., "Mon: $180.00 | Tue: $90.00 → $270.00")

![Week Total](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/10-week-total.png)

---

## Step 12: Add Dates & Notes

1. Select the **Week starting** date.
2. The Dates & Notes section shows the dates for each day.
3. You can adjust individual dates if needed.
4. Add any **Notes** that should appear on the invoice.

> **Tip:** Dates automatically populate based on your selected week. Click a day tile in Dates & Notes to cycle through states (Off → Full → Half).

---

## Step 13: Add to Invoice & Save Draft

1. Click **Add to invoice** to add the wage line item.
2. Verify the line item appears in the invoice (description: "Wages/Retainer — Week of...").
3. Click **Save draft** to save without sending.

> **Tip:** Saving as a draft lets you come back and edit later.

![Save Draft](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/11-save-draft.png)

---

## Step 14: View Your Invoices

1. Click **Dashboard** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see a list of all your invoices.
3. Click any invoice to view it.

![Invoice History](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/12-invoice-history.png)

---

## Step 15: Edit a Draft Invoice

1. From the dashboard, click the invoice you want to edit.
2. On the invoice view page, you can:
   - Continue editing
   - Download PDF
   - Send & email

> **Note:** Only drafts can be edited. Sent invoices are locked.

---

## Step 16: Download the PDF

1. Open the invoice (draft or sent).
2. Click **Download PDF**.
3. The PDF downloads to your computer.

> **Tip:** The PDF shows your company details, work dates, and payment information.

![PDF Download](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/14-pdf-download.png)

---

## Step 17: Save & Email

1. Complete your invoice with wage details.
2. Click **Save & email**.
3. The system:
   - Saves the invoice
   - Downloads the PDF
   - Opens an email with the invoice details pre-filled

> **Warning:** Make sure your bank details (Payment Details) are complete before sending. Invoices without payment details may delay processing.

![Save & Email](docs/screenshots/invoice-hub/13-save-email.png)

---

|-----|----------|----------|-----|

| **Tuesday** | $180 | $90 | $0 |
| **Wednesday** | $180 | $90 | $0 |
| **Thursday** | $180 | $90 | $0 |
| **Friday** | $180 | $90 | $0 |
| **Saturday** | — | $100 | $0 |

### Common Examples

| Scenario | Calculation | Total |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Mon–Fri Full | 5 × $180 | **$900** |
| Mon–Fri Full + Saturday | 5 × $180 + $100 | **$1000** |
| Monday Full + Tuesday Half | $180 + $90 | **$270** |
| Mon–Fri Half | 5 × $90 | **$450** |
| Monday Half + Saturday | $90 + $100 | **$190** |

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Tab** — move between fields
- **Enter** — submit form (when focused on a button)

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check your PIN is 4 digits. Contact admin if locked out. |
| "Payment Details: Missing information" | Go to My Profile → fill in bank name, BSB, account number. |
| Can't add to invoice | Make sure at least one day is selected (not Off). |
| Invoice won't save | Check that customer name and at least one line item exist. |
| PDF looks wrong | Check your personal details and payment info in My Profile. |
